Digital & Smart Classroom Facility

Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan is equipped with digital smart boards in every classroom, from Pre-KG to Class 10. This modern infrastructure ensures that students benefit from interactive and engaging lessons across all grades.

By combining traditional teaching methods with advanced digital resources, we aim to optimize classroom learning and prepare students for the demands of a technology-driven world.

Computer Laboratory Facility

Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan maintains a modern computer lab with 30 systems, giving students individual access for hands-on learning. An exclusive computer syllabus ensures they stay aligned with evolving technology trends, combining theory with practical skills.

Guided by trained faculty, the lab helps students build digital literacy, problem-solving abilities, and prepares them for a technology-driven future.

School Transportation Facility

Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan offers a reliable and well-organized school bus service for students across Mysuru. Our transportation network is designed to cover all major residential areas, ensuring safe and convenient travel to and from school.

Parents who wish to avail this facility can opt in during the admission process or at the start of the academic year. Each bus is supervised by trained staff and adheres to strict safety protocols, providing peace of mind for families and a comfortable commute for students.

Library

Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan offers a well‑stocked library with over 3,000 books, providing students access to a wide range of educational resources. The collection includes textbooks, reference materials, magazines, and general reading to support both academic learning and personal growth.

The library features comfortable seating arrangements, creating a quiet and welcoming space where students can read, research, and explore knowledge beyond the classroom.

Science Lab

Science is a universal subject that examines the structure and behaviour of the physical and natural world through observation and experiment. The Science lab at Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan aids a student in establishing the relevance of the theory. It brings clarity on the basic concept of the subject. Learning about the scientific method teaches children how to ask questions and gather the evidence needed for the most complete answer.

Campus Surveillance and Safety

At Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, the safety and well-being of our students and staff are of utmost importance. To ensure a secure learning environment, all 38 classrooms are equipped with CCTV cameras, providing continuous monitoring throughout the school day. In addition to classrooms, key areas such as corridors, entry and exit points, playgrounds, and administrative zones are also under surveillance.

This comprehensive security system helps deter misconduct, supports emergency response, and fosters a sense of accountability across the campus. By integrating technology into our safety protocols, we aim to create a protected space where students can focus on learning and personal growth with peace of mind.
