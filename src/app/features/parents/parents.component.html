Science Lab

Science is a universal subject that examines the structure and behaviour of the physical and natural world through observation and experiment. The Science lab at Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan aids a student in establishing the relevance of the theory. It brings clarity on the basic concept of the subject. Learning about the scientific method teaches children how to ask questions and gather the evidence needed for the most complete answer. Comprehensive science education enables students to develop new ideas, discover the world around them, and ask challenging questions.
